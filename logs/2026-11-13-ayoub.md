## Info gathered
- The top stone sits on the middle one: seen from above it stays over it until a minimum rotation angle. For a swing around the hub: radius 160 → 90°, 200 → 78°, 250 → 66°, 300 → 57°. **90° is the exact minimum at radius 160**, so the end stop needs 92–95° of margin.
- Turning around the centre, a rectangular robot's corners hit the stone at every angle (base_330 330 × 280 included). A round Ø300 robot clears (942 mm perimeter at start). With both stacks counted at start: ~1720 mm, so the concept depends on FAQ Q1.
- A standing stone (320 mm) must have its centre at 160–190 mm to stay ≥ table and ≤ 350 mm [GR F.3]. Top shaft: down 110; middle: none; bottom: up 110 (base +10).
- Helix groove on the hub collides (the stone must be clear of the stack, i.e. past 90°, before it moves in Z). Alternative B: off-centre cups (top 110 off one way, bottom the other), the spin does the height change; peak 349 mm for the top shaft.
- Parts to reuse: MG90S 📦, mini pumps 📦 (6), T8 + NEMA 17 + Ø8 rods 📦; elastics, torsion springs, 12 V valves 🇹🇳.
