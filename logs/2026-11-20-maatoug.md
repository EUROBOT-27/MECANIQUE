## Info gathered
- grail2 as drawn: lift 275 mm without the guide, 207 mm with it (−44 … +163); flip −44° … +156°, **180° blocked** by
  the lead screw (magnet hits it from +158°).
- grail2 problems: 365.6 mm from motor bottom to screw top (> 350 whatever the mounting); coupling CXJH D25 has Ø5
  bores for an Ø8 screw (5→8 coupler from the printer fixes it); lead 8 not self-locking; nut mount has 1 mm walls;
  unmated 4-pad servo arm.
- A belt (or any straight) lift alone can't do L4 on our 300 × 300 robot: the fork + carriage need ~360 mm of axis
  height, and the Grail still has to move inward over the roof (CDC D1/D3). So the belt replaces the screw in the
  lift + flip (v3 architecture).
- v4 first run (2020 column): same path as v3, worst margin 2.5 mm with the Grail, cycle 10.7 s, no clash; belt loop
  586 mm (cut ~650); motor top z 347.3 (limit 350).
- No free CAD of SCS8UU / SK8 without an account (GrabCAD, Cults3D); the FreeCAD parts library only has LM8UU.
