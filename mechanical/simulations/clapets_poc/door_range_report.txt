Physical range: -47.0 .. +94.0 deg = 141.0 deg of door travel.
Servo limit: 180 deg servo / x2 gears = 90 deg of door travel.
-> the servo, not the mechanism, limits the door: choose a 90 deg window inside the physical range, e.g. -47 .. +43 deg (keeps open -21 and clamp +2.8).

Note: the fold limit (+94 deg) is the pads reaching the RESERVED space of the future suction head, not a part;
the open limit (-47 deg) is the upper door arm touching its own door unit.
