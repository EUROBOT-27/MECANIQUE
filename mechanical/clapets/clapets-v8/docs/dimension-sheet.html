  • Folded, the door tips stop at x ±30, inside the 300 × 300 base: start perimeter 1200.0 mm (GR F.3 limit 1200).
  • Open, each door reaches x ≈ ±225 but stays behind the stack face (y ≤ 137), so the robot can drive up to a quarry with the doors open. Deployed perimeter ≈ 1367 mm (limit 1400).
  • On the robot, CAD x is the robot's Y (sideways) and CAD y is the robot's X (driving direction). Modules sit on the front and back faces.

Turning the stack: heights live

Lift the stack, then turn it about the head axis. Doors are open (−38°) and the cups hold the stack.

Head axis Stack top Stack lowest corner Head carrier top

The turn needs 75 mm of lift so the stack corners (half-diagonal 229.8 mm) clear the table by 10 mm at 45°. The stack then reaches z 470, above 350. GR F.3 limits manipulated objects to 350 mm; G E.1 lets game elements exceed it in construction zones. Turning is only legal there if FAQ Q9 is answered "yes".

new

The full robot model places the clapets 25 mm too high

The clapets are designed with the socle on the table (z 0 = table). robot/fc/layout.py sets CLAPETS_Z0 = 25 (socle on the base plate), so in ROBOT.FCStd the cups sit at z 110 / 190 / 270 instead of 85 / 165 / 245, off the stone centres. The top of the turn lift becomes 372.7, over 350. Either the socle goes down through the base plate (motor and socle on the table side), or the module is redesigned 25 mm shorter. Switch the height chart to "in the robot model" to see it.

open

Rails and top tie must be cut

The 2020 rails reach z 356 and the top tie z 366 (381 and 391 in the robot model). They also clash with the beacon support in robot_report.txt. The lift needs about 75 mm of travel above the carriage, so check the cut length against the lift.

check

Start perimeter in the robot report: 1200.3 mm

The report prints "OK" but 1200.3 is 0.3 mm over the GR F.3 limit, and the overall box is y ±153.6, so something sticks out past the 300 × 300 footprint.

open

Lift travel: 75 mm or under 20 mm?

The cycle uses 75 mm to turn the stack; Seif mentioned less than 20 mm. Decide before cutting the lead screw (303 mm now, z 60–363).

sync

v8 still uses some old v7 numbers

v8's class G still has the horn top at 45.7 (measured 47.4 in v7, door gears +1.7 mm), motor body 38 (measured 37) and insert holes 6.5 deep (v7: 7.0). Module A changes from v7 (2026-11-08 to 11-10) are not carried into v8 yet.

rules

FAQ Q1 and Q9 still open

Q1: does a carried stack count in the perimeter? The design assumes no. Q9: may a held stone go above 350 mm in a construction zone? Turning the stack depends on it.
