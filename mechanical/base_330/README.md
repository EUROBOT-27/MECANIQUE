# base_330 — 330 mm robot base plate (FreeCAD)

> **Status: temporary base.** This base is an **alternative to the JSUMO wheels** (D-004) until we get them.
> It is built around the wheels we already have (`roue-90mm avec axe40mm`, Ø90, 20 mm tyre) so the
> **navigation team can proceed with their work** (driving, odometry with the encoder wheels, tuning) on a real
> chassis now. When the JSUMO wheels arrive, the wheel pocket, encoder rods and lift height must be re-checked
> for them (all are parameters in `base_330.py`).

## What it is

A 330 × 280 × 5 mm base plate for a differential-drive robot, laser-cut or machined from the DXF:

- **Drive:** 2 × Pololu 37D gear motors (no encoder) mounted back to back on the centre line, each on a hinge
  bracket raised by a 6 mm MDF lift, driving a 90 mm wheel through a coupler. The drive axle passes through the
  plate centre.
- **Odometry:** 2 spring-loaded encoder wheel units (`sysRoueCodeusee`), one outside each drive wheel, on Ø8 rods
  pressed into the plate.
- **Support:** 2 caster balls (front and back, on the Y axis), set 2 mm above the floor so the drive wheels carry
  the weight.
- **Structure:** 4 corner holes (Ø6.35) for the side-wall profiles; 2 × Ø5.5 per end for the back-wall profiles (M5).
- **Clapets v7 mount:** 4 × Ø4.8 holes for the module A socle, on both ends (8 holes in all, see below).
- Fully symmetric about both axes.

Parametric, symmetric rework of `base - Copy.SLDPRT`. Generated by `base_330.py` (parameters at the top).

## Clapets v7 mount (2026-11-12)

The clapets v7 mechanism (`../clapets-v7/`, module A) is **unchanged**. Only holes were added to the base.
`A_SOCLE` bolts down with 4 × M4 (its holes are Ø4.4 at x ±63, y 18 / 53 in the module frame).

- **Position:** module frame x = base x; the module sits **23 mm forward** (`CLAP_DY`). The stack face is 23 mm in
  front of the plate edge (y 163), and the socle covers y 31.5…83.5. At 0 mm (stack face on the plate edge), the
  socle, motor plate, rails and NEMA sit on the drive motors and lifts, and the door units hit the wheels.
  19 mm clears them by 3 mm. 23 mm also keeps the socle 2 mm off the lift strap slots (outer edge y 29.5).
- **Holes:** Ø4.8 at (±63, 41) and (±63, 76), mirrored to −Y so the plate stays symmetric (the module can go on either
  end). Ø4.8 = M4 loose clearance (ISO 273 coarse): it absorbs the printed socle's hole error and PLA shrinkage
  over the 126 mm pitch (~0.4 mm).
- **Fasteners:** 4 × M4 × 20 button head from the top, washer under the head and under the nut (socle 6 + plate 5 +
  2 × 0.8 + nut 3.2 = 15.8 → ~4 mm past the nut, the tip ends ~5 mm above the table).
- **Assembly:** `base_330_assembly.py` moves `MODULE_A_CLAPETS.step` as one block by (0, 23, 18.5), so the socle
  stands on the plate top.
- **Checked (FreeCAD, module A STEPs on this plate):** socle and plate hole axes coincide. Closest part to the
  drive is the socle, at 7.5 mm (then NEMA 11.0, door units 12.0, motor plate 18.3). Socle on the plate with no
  overlap, M4 screws clear of the socle and plate holes. The drive is modelled as
  wheels Ø90 × 20, motors Ø37 and lift + hinge blocks.
- **Clash: front corner profiles.** A 2020 profile standing on a front corner hole (±154, 129) is hit by the lower
  door arm from open (−38°) to +22° (all working poses), and the 28T gear is 0.45 mm from it. Don't put profiles
  on the front corner holes with the clapets mounted. The corner holes are for the side walls; the test robot has
  none (2026-11-12), so nothing stands there for now. Move them before walls are added.
- **Still to check:** the encoder wheel units (`sysRoueCodeusee`, not in the repo) against the door units (door unit
  bottom at y ≥ 57, z 27 above the table).
- **Height:** with the socle on the plate top (18.5 above the table), the whole mechanism is 18.5 mm higher than in
  its table-referenced CAD. Check the 350 mm height limit (top tie at 366 + 18.5 today; the v7 README already plans
  to cut the profiles) and the door / cup heights against the stones.

## Back wall: electronics + LiPo (2026-11-12)

For the test robot (no side walls), the electronics and the LiPo go on a wall across the rear of the base.

- **Profiles:** 2 × 2020, **200 mm**, standing at (±100, −130) with the outer face flush with the rear edge
  (y −140…−120). Each one is held by an **M5 × 12** button head (+ washer) from under the plate into its tapped
  centre bore. Base holes Ø5.5 (mirrored to +Y; under the clapets they are only holes). Two profiles, not one:
  one M5 lets a profile turn, and one 20 mm face can't hold a wall with a LiPo on it. Check the profile ends are
  tapped M5 (the Ender profiles are, the lab offcuts have a "tapped centre bore": confirm the size).
- **Wall:** 5 mm MDF, 260 × 200 (x ±130), on the profiles' inner face (y −120…−115), standing on the plate. Top
  218.5 mm above the table. Bolted with **4 × M5 × 10 button head + T-nut** (slot 6), 2 per profile at 25 and 175
  above the plate. The wall holes are **horizontal slots 5.5 wide, ±1.5 mm**: they take up the position error of
  the base holes, the profile on its single screw and the laser cut (heights are free: the T-nuts slide).
- **Zip-tie pegboard:** 99 × Ø4.5 holes on a 20 mm grid (x −120…120, 20…180 above the plate). Any two holes make
  a tie pair 20, 40, 60… mm apart. Ties up to 3.6 mm wide go through; M4 screws and PCB standoffs fit too. No
  holes behind the profiles (x ±100).
- **LiPo straps:** 4 vertical slots 22 × 3 for a 20 mm Velcro strap, at x ±70 (140 apart: packs up to ~130 mm
  long), centred 30 and 110 above the plate. Low pair = LiPo low, standing on the plate against the wall (low centre
  of gravity, design principles). Strap it so it comes out without tools (battery swap between matches).
- **Bottom notch** 60 × 10 (x ±30): clears the rear caster's flange and its 2 screws (x ±20). The flange size is
  not in the CAD: if the real flange is bigger, widen `BW_NOTCH` and regenerate.
- **Checked (assembly):** wall and profiles sit on the plate with no overlap. Wall 70 mm from the drive
  (placeholders), 136 mm from the clapets. Wall top 218.5 is well under 350.
- Parameters: `PROF_*` and `BW_*` in `base_330.py`.

## Lift

A spacer between the plate and each hinge, **6 mm = two 3 mm MDF layers**.
Both layers have the same outline: 52 × 48 mm (x 16…68, y −24…24), R3 corners. Both lifts are the same part.

- 4 × Ø3.5 hinge holes (x 26.9 / 50.9, y ±15): **M3 × 20** from the top through hinge + lift + plate, nut under the plate.
- The lift is also strapped down through the two slots of the plate (as in the original base); it sits between them.
- Total: 4 pieces of 3 mm MDF, 8 × M3×20, 8 nuts (+ washers under the nuts against the MDF).
- Assembly order: the hinge bolts sit under the motor body → bolt hinge + lift + plate first, then fit the motor.

Why 6 mm: the caster ball (mounted down through the Ø33 hole, flange on the plate) reaches 16.5 mm below the plate
top, the motor shaft is 20.5 mm above the hinge seat and the wheel radius is 45. An 8 mm lift would put the casters
exactly on the floor; 6 mm leaves them **2 mm above the floor** so the drive wheels always carry the weight
(design principle "casters ~2 mm above the floor line"). The plate is then 13.5 mm above the floor (1 mm lower than
the old assembly). **Measure the MDF**: the 3 + 3 stack should not come out over 8 mm.

## Checked (real parts placed around the plate, FreeCAD)

- No interference: motors, hinges, couplers, wheels, encoders, casters, lifts.
- Hinge, encoder-rod and caster holes line up with the parts (≤ 0.01 mm). Caster body Ø32 in the Ø33 hole.
- Floor: wheels on the floor, casters 2.0 mm above it.
- Clearances: tyre ↔ pocket 5 mm, tyre ↔ encoder 5 mm, encoder wheel ↔ notch 2.4 mm.
- Design principles (`mechanical/design-principles.md`): symmetry, drive axle through the centre, low plate,
  spring-loaded encoder wheels, casters 2 mm up. Not yet: designed cable routing (no cable holes / zip-tie anchors).

- The rest of the robot (walls, `etage`, cover, profiles) is still sized for the 290 plate.
- The plate is 1 mm closer to the floor than in the old assembly, so each encoder spring is compressed 1 mm more:
  check it still has travel.
- Motor dimensions come from `MCC.SLDPRT` (shaft offset 6.5, shaft 13.5 past the face), not from a datasheet:
  measure the real GB37-30 and update if it differs.
- Assumption: "width" = the wheel-axis direction (290 → 330). If it was the other side, change `W`/`L`.
