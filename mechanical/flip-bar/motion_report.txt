== Torque (quasi-static, gravity; masses from the CAD: bar assembly 0.323, crank 0.076, rocker 0.093, stones 3 x 0.090 kg)
   with 3 stones            crank: lift peak +0.66 N.m (s 0.21), brake peak -0.82 N.m (s 1.00); sign changes at s 0.53
   empty (stones released)  crank: lift peak +0.40 N.m (s 0.21), brake peak -0.49 N.m (s 1.00); sign changes at s 0.53
   unpowered (gears back-drive): with the torque > 0 the bar falls back toward the start, < 0 it falls toward
   upright -> rests on the hard stop there. The stops take the full load: 0.86 N.m with stones.

== Drive: 17HS4401S, 2-stage spur 15T/45T x 2 = 9:1, module 1.0, 9 mm wide, printed ABS, efficiency 0.81
   motor: holding 0.40 N.m at 1.7 A (datasheet, CHECK the label); driven at ~1.0 A -> usable 0.20 N.m up to 300 rpm (assumed)
   resolution: 16 microsteps -> 0.0125 deg of crank per microstep (80 microsteps per crank degree)
   flip in 0.6 s (trapezoid, 25% ramps): motor up to 400 rpm, peak motor torque 0.172 N.m, worst margin x1.01 at s 0.17 TIGHT
   flip in 1.0 s (trapezoid, 25% ramps): motor up to 240 rpm, peak motor torque 0.118 N.m, worst margin x1.70 at s 0.17 OK
