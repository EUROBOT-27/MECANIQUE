== Torque (quasi-static, gravity; masses from the CAD: bar assembly 0.358, crank 0.081, rocker 0.093, stones 3 x 0.090 kg)
   with 3 stones            crank: lift peak +0.69 N.m (s 0.21), brake peak -0.86 N.m (s 1.00); sign changes at s 0.53
   empty (stones released)  crank: lift peak +0.44 N.m (s 0.21), brake peak -0.53 N.m (s 1.00); sign changes at s 0.53
   unpowered (gears back-drive): with the torque > 0 the bar falls back toward the start, < 0 it falls toward
   upright -> rests on the hard stop there. The stops take the full load: 0.91 N.m with stones.
