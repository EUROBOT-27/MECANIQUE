# Centre column / rings for the electronics (idea, Seif 2026-11-20)

**Status: idea, not a decision.** Came up while anchoring the top of the Grail Flipper's column on base_330_js
(`../Grail_v2/README.md`): its top 2020 cross-member (z 260–280) runs inward to the robot's centre and needs
something to bolt to.

Two ways, to choose when the front / back modules are fixed:
1. **Centre column:** a vertical profile (or two) in the middle of the base, between the drive motors' tops and the
   roof. It takes the Flipper's top cross-member, the roof, maybe the beacon support mast.
2. **Rings on the front and back modules' profiles:** if the front and back faces use vertical 2020 profiles (the
   clapets lifts' rails), printed or laser-cut **rings / frames clamp onto them**, and carry **floors (étages)** for the
   electronics, battery and wiring. The Flipper's top cross-member bolts to one of these frames.

Either way it gives the robot a stiff top frame (roof + Flipper + beacon support) and a place for the electronics
stack, which layout-v1 put in a narrow core slab (x ±64, y ±18).

To check when it is designed: the Flipper's moving volume (NEMA at x 89–131, y −70…−11, z 110–272; column x 118–138,
y −6…14), the Grail's roof spot (x 93–163, y ±35, z ≥ 306), the beacon support (Ø ≤ 100 near the centre, top 430), the
e-stop and the battery swap (GR F.4.b).
