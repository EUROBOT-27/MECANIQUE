# Clapets module: redesign around our parts (v1 + v2)

Status: **proposal** (Claude, 2026-10-27), nothing decided yet. Source designs (not edited):
`C:\Projects\EUROBOT\Mecanique\module_clapets` (v1) and `module_clapets_v2` (v2). Their READMEs hold the full
geometry and checks; CAD is generated by the C# scripts in their `sw/` folders. `module_clapets_v3` and the
`base omniwheel 4/V2_280` base are set aside (Maatoug, 2026-10-27).

## 1. What v1 and v2 are (short)

- **v1:** one module per driving face, front + rear → **2 quarry stacks**. Two servo doors with foam pads clamp the
  110 × 110 box ends; a backstop plate stops the stack tipping; the whole door block rides on a carriage that a
  NEMA17 + TR8×2 screw lifts **15 mm** (rule: ≤ 20 mm lift for a 330 mm wall, [GR F.3]). Checked: start
  1174 mm, worst case 1387 mm (≤ 1400), all heights ≤ 350 mm.
- **v2:** v1 doors + a **suction head** in the middle (6 × DP-S30, TPU spring membrane, 3 × 12 V pumps + 3 vent valves).
  The head holds the 3 boxes, the doors open, the head lifts and **turns the stack 90°** → 3 standing boxes =
  3 towers, released one by one. Second NEMA17 lift for the head, third MG995 to turn.
- Both were designed for the **V2 280 omni octagon**. Our base is **differential (D-004)**: the v1 README says the
  interface (3 × M4 standoffs at 70 mm, module strip 75 mm deep on the face) transfers to a diff base, and
  that front/rear strips are *easier* there because the wheels sit mid-side.

## 3. Proposed changes

### 3.1 Lift: reuse the Ender Z axis (biggest change)
The Ender Z axis *is* a ready vertical lift: 2040 upright + Z carriage with V-wheels + NEMA17 + coupler + T8 screw
+ brass nut + the black nut bracket (photo `c349f2d1`). Proposal for v1: **replace the 2 rods + 4 LM8UU with one
2040 rail (cut to ~260 mm) and a V-wheel carriage**; the door block bolts to the carriage plate.

- ✅ No import, no LM8UU/integrated-screw motor to buy. Rail + wheels resist tilt well, and the rail is a stiff spine
  for the module.
- ⚠ **Lead 8 mm is not self-locking** (v1 chose TR8×2 for that). With lead 8 the stack would drop 15 mm when the
  motor is unpowered. Options: (a) keep the stepper energised all match (holding torque ≫ needed; dropping 15 mm
  at the end/e-stop is harmless, the stack just lands); (b) find a lead-2 screw among the lab screws (measure:
  mm per turn); (c) buy TR8×2 locally. Recommended: **(a)**, check (b) first.
- ⚠ **Height:** a separate motor + coupler is ~25 mm taller than an integrated screw. v1 (motor top 286) → ~311: OK.
  v2 (crossbar 300, motor top 346) → ~371: **over 350**. v2 needs the motor below the crossbar beside the screw
  with a GT2 belt (Ender pulleys/belt, 📦 except an 8 mm-bore pulley), or a lower crossbar.
- Faster too: 15 mm = ~2 turns instead of 7.5.

### 3.2 Door hinges: 608ZZ + library rods
688ZZ is rare locally; 608ZZ is everywhere. Wing bearing seats go from Ø16 × 5 to Ø22 × 7; wing plates get
~3 mm wider around the hinge. Check the perimeter worst case again (13 mm margin today). First **read the codes on our
bearings** (photos `a45eb9fd`, `b6e9494c`): if some are 688/608, use them.

### 3.3 Servos: MG995 → MG996R
Same body and flange, so `SUPPORT_SERVO` doesn't change. Measure one of ours (flange height 26.6 mm, horn top 43.5 mm
are the values the gears are placed from) and count how many we have.

### 3.4 Pumps (v2): re-layout for the real size
v2 lays 3 pumps side by side 26.5 mm apart (Ø25 modelled). Real pumps are **Ø31.8 × 66**: 3 abreast need ≥ ~105 mm.
(v3 already uses a 31 × 66 pump model, so its layout can be borrowed.)

### 3.5 Fixings (choice for you)
v1/v2 use **captive steel M3 nuts, no heat-set inserts**. We have a box of brass inserts. Proposal: keep nuts for
anything structural (wings ↔ centre, carriage, servo flanges), allow inserts for covers, switches, cable clips.

### 3.6 Base interface: differential base, not V2 280
Keep the v1 interface (3 standoffs, Z_IF 70 mm, module strip 75 mm deep). Needs: **the diff base outline and wheel
positions** → then rerun the perimeter/height checks in `clapets.py` with `BASE` set to it.

## 4. Conflicts with the hub (to settle before CAD)
1. **FAQ Q1 (stones in the perimeter):** both designs assume carried stones don't count. If they count, 2 stacks
   are impossible (1654 mm). Already our top open FAQ.
2. **v2 turn above 350 mm:** the stack top reaches 474 mm while turning. [G E.1] lets game elements exceed 350 mm
   in zones/castle (actuators still ≤ 350: v2 keeps them ≤ 346); [GR F.3] says manipulated objects ≤ 350 mm.
   That is exactly **FAQ Q9**. v2 is only legal if Q9 is answered "yes".
3. **Gate (D-011):** the gate must share a driving face with a stone gripper. With clapets on both faces, the gate
   slot (~340 × 130 mm) has to live inside one clapets module. v2's head could become the gate mechanism (its
   README notes: needs head axis ~380 for the lintel: check against the 350 mm actuator limit).
4. **Build order (D-010):** walls first. v1 builds walls directly (a quarry stack *is* a wall). v2 (towers) is
   "2nd" priority. Suggest: **build v1-R first, keep v2's head as an add-on.**

## 4b. PoC (2026-10-27): second version, after Maatoug's review
First PoC (one 2040 rail + crossbar motor) was **wiped**: D-shaft hinges not available, stepper floating on a
cantilevered crossbar, backstop judged unnecessary, too big, and the lift screw sat in the centre where the
suction head goes. **PoC 2** (`C:\Projects\EUROBOT\Mecanique\module_clapets_poc\README.md`):
- Lift = the **Two Trees printer's Y axis stood on end**: 2 rails as uprights, the **butterfly bed plate + its 4
  V-wheels as the carriage** (replaces `CHARIOT_CENTRE`). NEMA17 standing in a printed base, coupler, T8 screw up the
  middle *behind* the plate, 608 at the top.
- Doors: **fixed plain Ø8 shaft**, hub on 2× 608ZZ with the 32T gear printed on it, MG996R upside down under the top
  plate. No backstop; longer doors (pads at the box centre).
- **Suction head reserved block** in front of the plate centre; the head will bolt to the plate (rides with the doors).
- Printer parts are **placeholders** until measured (no CAD exists for the Two Trees): list in the PoC README.
